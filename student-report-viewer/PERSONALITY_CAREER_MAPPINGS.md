# Career-Personality Mappings Specification

This document details how each futuristic career maps to the 6 STELLAR personality dimensions.

---

## Mapping Legend

**Ideal Score Range**: 0-100 scale
- 0-30: Strong preference for left pole
- 31-49: Moderate preference for left pole
- 50: Balanced/neutral
- 51-69: Moderate preference for right pole
- 70-100: Strong preference for right pole

**Trait Weight**: How important this dimension is for the career
- 1 = Normal importance
- 2 = Important
- 3 = Critical

**Dimensions**:
- EI: Explorer (0) ↔ Investigator (100)
- SC: Social (0) ↔ Concentrated (100)
- PT: Practical (0) ↔ Theoretical (100)
- RN: Routine (0) ↔ Novel (100)
- AD: Analytical (0) ↔ Decisive (100)
- LG: Local/Detail (0) ↔ Global/Big Picture (100)
